Adaptation: SHERLOQ exports FP32 ONNX operators from the supplied checkpoint,
preserves learned parameters and global attention, and makes geometric feature
splits and final interpolation depend on the original input dimensions.
Reflection padding to multiples of32 is cropped from the native final output.
Operator partitioning, bounded row windows and temporary feature storage do
not replace the image with independently inferred tiles. This adaptation is
distinct from the original fixed512x512 evaluation protocol. The authors do
not endorse this adaptation.
